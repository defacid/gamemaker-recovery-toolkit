# Legal and safety boundary

This toolkit is for authorized preservation, interoperability, archival
research, and recovery of software you own or have permission to examine.

- Do not publish recovered game assets or source without the rights holder's
  permission.
- Do not bundle GameMaker runtimes, third-party decompilers, system fonts, or
  proprietary SDK files in this repository.
- Treat archived executables as untrusted input. Prefer static unpacking and
  parsing in an isolated environment.
- Keep original artifacts immutable and record cryptographic hashes.
- Keep generated projects and browser exports separate from preservation
  originals.
- Review the applicable license and local law before distributing a converted
  game.

The scripts provide technical mechanisms, not permission to use third-party
content.
